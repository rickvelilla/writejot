About Writejot

Writejot was created to make it easier for students to find and share useful study materials.

  • Students can access a catalog of study notes.
  • Students can submit their own notes for review.
  • Submitted notes are reviewed before being added to the catalog.
  • Catalog access is controlled through the student's account status.
  • Subscription payment is currently handled in person.
